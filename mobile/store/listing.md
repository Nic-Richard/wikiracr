## Full description

Race through Wikipedia from a start article to a goal article by clicking links.

Find a route, watch your clicks and see how quickly you can reach the goal. Play a
solo race, take on the Daily Challenge or join a multiplayer room with friends.

- Navigate real Wikipedia articles through their links.
- Try different routes and compare your results.
- Race the daily article pair.
- Play multiplayer and compete on leaderboards.
- Sign in to keep account-linked progress and results.

An optional monthly WikiRacr Pro subscription adds extra modes and custom game
options. Your local Google Play price is shown before purchase. Subscriptions renew
automatically unless cancelled; manage Android subscriptions in Google Play.
Core guest races do not require a subscription. An internet connection is required.

Wikipedia content comes from Wikipedia and its contributors. WikiRacr is an
independent game and is not affiliated with the Wikimedia Foundation.
